Luoyang Anxin & Wuhan Lixin New Energy Technology Co., Ltd.

Luoyang Anxin New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. and Wuhan Lixin New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. are high-tech enterprises specializing in the fields of primary batteries and secondary rechargeable batteries. The company has long been deeply involved in the technical research, product development, large-scale manufacturing, and global sales of primary batteries and secondary rechargeable batteries. Relying on continuous technological innovation and strict quality control, it provides customers with safe, efficient, stable, and reliable energy products and integrated energy solutions. By empowering the Internet of Everything and the development of the smart industry with professional energy technology, it helps various fields achieve more efficient and sustainable energy supply and intelligent applications.
